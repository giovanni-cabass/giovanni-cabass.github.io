## Bio

I am a cosmologist working on the early universe and large-scale structure (LSS). I am currently Research Staff at the Institut Ruđer Bošković in Zagreb, after postdocs at the Max Planck Institute for Astrophysics in Garching and the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton. My main contribution to cosmology was putting the first constraints on the microphysics of inflation from LSS data. Over the past few years I have been strongly pulled towards agentic research, which let me pursue my interest in treating the latent spaces of neural networks as genuine differential-geometric objects, and in bringing a physicist's approach to financial time series. One of my main current research lines is implementing reproducible, agent-verified workflows to address problems in theoretical cosmology.
